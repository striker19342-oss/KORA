# KORA
KORA is a bidder-first sealed-bid auction dApp. It proves a bid meets an auction's public eligibility rule while keeping the amount, bidder secret, and identity out of public records.

## Discovery
| Concept | Pitch | Why not selected |
|---|---|---|
| QuietBallot | Anonymous governance voting | Strong privacy need, but less bidder-oriented |
| KORA | A sealed auction where the amount stays private | Selected: immediate, visible proof value |
| ShiftVeil | Private payroll split verification | More complex onboarding for a one-minute MVP |
